## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which vape detector has the lowest false alarm rate?

Detectors that use AI/ML classification (Mistio, Verkada) have lower false alarm rates than threshold-based systems. The key differentiator is whether the system was specifically trained on common bathroom products like cologne, body spray, and cleaning solutions.

### Do I need WiFi for a vape detector?

Most detectors require WiFi or PoE (which provides both power and network). Mistio uses cellular connectivity and does not require access to the school's WiFi network, which simplifies IT approval and network security concerns.

### How many vape detectors does a school need?

One per bathroom is the standard recommendation. A typical high school with 20 bathrooms needs 20 sensors. Add sensors to locker rooms, stairwells, and known problem areas as budget allows.

### Can vape detectors be defeated by students?

All detectors can be partially circumvented (exhaling into clothing, out a window). Ceiling-mounted sensors in small bathrooms are the hardest to defeat because aerosol disperses throughout the space within seconds regardless of direction.
